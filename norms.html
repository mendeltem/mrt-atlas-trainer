Brain volume norms

How large is a normal thalamus, hippocampus or cortex, in women and in men? Median volumes from 452 healthy adults, measured with SynthSeg, plus an answer to whether SynthSeg on real 3D scans belongs in the trainer.

SynthSeg in the trainer: yes, as a second mode

Keep the MNI152 template as the clean default for learning names. It is an average of 152 brains, smooth and symmetric, and every atlas is drawn on it. A second mode with real single brains, segmented by SynthSeg, adds what the template cannot show: how much anatomy varies between people, how a brain looks at 25 and at 80, and what volume each structure has.

1 · Pick real scans

Use healthy, openly licensed 3D T1 scans with age and sex. DLBS (CC0) fits best: it was segmented and QC-checked for this page. IXI (CC BY-SA 3.0) is the alternative.

2 · SynthSeg, then render

mri_synthseg --parc --robust gives 32 structures plus 68 cortex regions (Desikan–Killiany) in the scan's own space. Render slices in Python like the MNI view.

3 · Atlas on top, optional

AAL or Harvard-Oxford need a warp from MNI into the person (SynthMorph or ANTs). It works, but the borders are only approximate. SynthSeg's own labels are more exact in that brain.

4 · Click shows the norm

Clicking a structure shows its volume next to the median for that sex and age band below. Learners see whether this person is typical.

How to read these numbers

Men have larger heads. Raw volumes are about 10–13 % larger in men, about as much as the intracranial volume. Switch to "% of ICV" and most differences shrink to a few percent.
Age matters more than sex. From 20–39 to 80+, the median hippocampus is about 13 % smaller, the thalamus about 23 % smaller, and the lateral ventricles more than three times as large. Always compare a person with their own age band.
Values depend on the tool. SynthSeg and FreeSurfer do not give the same ml. Measured on these same DLBS scans, the SynthSeg thalamus is 17 % smaller than FreeSurfer 5.3, while the hippocampus is about equal. Compare SynthSeg only with SynthSeg norms.
One sample, one scanner. DLBS is one US study at 3 T with cognitively healthy volunteers (MMSE ≥ 26). Broad, multi-site norms are in the sources: Potvin and brainchart.io.

Method: SynthSeg 2.0 (FreeSurfer 8.2.0 mri_synthseg --parc --robust) on the raw DLBS wave-1 MPRAGE.
